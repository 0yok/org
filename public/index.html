FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers, no jargon.

How do the 10 free messages work?+
Every account starts with 10 messages. Once you use them, you'll be prompted to redeem an unlimited key to keep going — your account and history stay exactly as they are.
Can I use my account on another device?+
Yes. Accounts are stored centrally, so logging in with the same username and password on any device gives you the same message count and unlock status.
What happens if I share my key?+
Each key can be redeemed exactly once, by whichever account uses it first. After that it's permanently invalid — so keys aren't meant to be shared.
Does the unlimited key remove all restrictions?+
It removes the message cap — you get unlimited messages and priority answer depth. It doesn't change what the assistant will or won't help with; illegal requests are declined regardless of tier.
